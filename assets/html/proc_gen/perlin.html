Control Map

Roam a 100 x 100 unit terrain starting in its northwest corner.

  • Mouse Drag to orbit & wheel to zoom
  • Shift + Mouse Drag to pan
  • W A S D Move camera across the plane
  • 1-9 Set active noise components
  • R Regenerate heightmap with a fresh seed
  • F Toggle smoothstep vs smootherstep shaping
  • P Show or hide the ground grid helper
  • Arrows Shift the sun along the X/Z axes
